# Distribution Reality Check — 2026 (governs all candidate scoring)

**Researched 2026-09-03. Evidence tier [S] = web-search result summary; [I] = inference.**
This is the backbone finding of the whole run. Distribution, not product, is the binding constraint.

## Finding 1 — Generic SEO is NOT a viable primary channel for a zero-authority site in 2026

- SparkToro (June 2026): **68.01% of US Google searches ended with no click** in Jan–Apr 2026, up from 60.45% in 2024. Clicks reaching the open web fell from 374 to **276 per 1,000 searches** — a 26% drop in two years. [S] https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click
- Pew Research: users click a traditional result **8% of the time when an AI Overview is present, vs 15% without**. [S] https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-2026-study-479717
- Google's Nov 2024 site-reputation-abuse and Mar 2024 scaled-content-abuse policies target exactly the cheap solo-founder playbook. Google reported the Mar 2024 update **deindexed 800+ sites and cut low-quality content 45%**. [S] https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2024/11/site-reputation-abuse
- Recovery is rare: of ~400 sites tracked after the Aug 2024 core update, **only ~22% recovered ≥20% of traffic**. [S] (via digitalapplied.com)

> **Consequence: any candidate whose primary channel is programmatic SEO or content marketing is effectively disqualified for this operator.** This kills an entire family from the brief's §5 search list. Reported as a finding, not a failure.

## Finding 2 — AI-assistant citation (GEO/AEO) is not yet a documented acquisition channel

AI referral traffic converts well *when it arrives* (Seer Interactive: 16% vs 1.8% for Google organic [S] seerinteractive.com), but absolute volume is a rounding error — ChatGPT worldwide referral share ~0.23–0.32% of traffic, Apr–May 2026 [S].
**NO EVIDENCE FOUND** of any solo founder documenting paying customers acquired via AI citation with real numbers. Treat GEO as a free byproduct of good content, never as a budgeted channel.

## Finding 4 — NZ COLD EMAIL IS CONSENT-BASED. This is a serious operator-specific constraint.

The **Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007** applies to messages sent *from* New Zealand regardless of recipient location. Unlike US CAN-SPAM (opt-out; cold email legal by default), **NZ law requires express, inferred, or deemed consent**. Penalties up to **NZ$500,000**. [S] https://www.legislation.govt.nz , https://www.dia.govt.nz , https://sprintlaw.co.nz

**Compliant route:** *deemed consent* — the recipient's business address is conspicuously published (e.g. their own website/staff page) AND the message is relevant to their role, with clear sender identification and a working unsubscribe.
**Non-compliant:** scraping a generic list and blasting it.

> **Consequence:** channel #2 remains available but must be built as *targeted, role-relevant outreach to publicly-listed business contacts* — which caps volume and rules out spray-and-pray. This should shape how any list is built. Confirm with a NZ lawyer before sending at volume.

## Finding 5 — Timeline evidence is thin and survivorship-biased

- One documented case: Everflow's founder took **6–7 months of discovery before the first two paying customers**, via a conference (in-person outbound). [S] saasclub.io
- Founder anecdotes consistently say the first customers came from **direct outreach and existing network**, not passive discovery. [S] indiehackers.com
- **Survivorship bias is severe:** only founders who succeeded write case studies. Founders who tried 12 months and got nowhere are structurally invisible. **Treat every published timeline as an optimistic upper bound.**
