# High-Price Niche B2B With Enumerable Buyers (2026-09-03)

[S] = search-result summary + URL; [I] = inference. All accessed 2026-09-03.

## 1. Carrier-fraud vetting for SMALL freight brokerages (1–10 staff) — US$300–800/mo
**Demand — the strongest dated evidence in the project:**
- US/Canada cargo-theft losses **~$725M in 2025, up 60% from $455M in 2024**; **$10B drained from freight 2022–2025** via double-brokering and non-payment [S] inboundlogistics.com
- FMCSA received **8,000+ double-brokering complaints in 2025, vs ~2,000 in 2021**; **15,419 broker operating authorities revoked Jan 2022–Dec 2025** (~59% of active broker authorities) [S] barchart.com (TIA statement)
- Live job ad for "Carrier Compliance Specialist" doing exactly this manually [S] indeed.com
**Competitors:** Highway (**no published pricing anywhere — demo-gated**); Truckstop RMIS (**Lite $340/mo published**, Premium quote-only); MyCarrierPortal (**acquired by Descartes for $24M upfront**, folded into enterprise stack); Carrier411 (~$100/mo, one complaint thread but **~2013 vintage — too old to cite as current**) [S]
**Enumerable? YES —** FMCSA Licensing & Insurance public database lists every licensed broker by name/MC number: https://li-public.fmcsa.dot.gov/LIVIEW/ ; plus TIA member directory
**Why now: strong and dated** — fraud and complaint volume both spiked in 2025; authority revocations actively reshaping the market
**Hours/wk:** ~8–12 [I]
**Caution:** Truckstop RMIS Lite at $340/mo already sits in the target price band.

## 2. Producer licence & CE compliance for small insurance agencies (5–30 producers) — US$300–700/mo
**Demand:**
- Trade press Aug 2026: "Producer compliance platforms face a new test on cost" [S] fintech.global
- Sircon users cite **$22.50/mo base plus per-agent cost**, "steep to small setups", one reviewer saying "the prices are insane on top of that" [S] sourceforge.net
- AgentSync: reviewer says "for the cost, we expected a more comprehensive and adaptable solution"; third-party procurement data cites **average annual contracts >$100k, up to ~$370k**, *before* the required Salesforce licensing [S] g2.com
- **Two live job ads** for "Insurance License and Compliance Coordinator" / "Insurance Licensing Coordinator" — the manual version being hired for [S] indeed.com
**Competitors:** AgentSync (quote-only, needs Salesforce, $100k+/yr); Sircon/Vertafore (base + per-producer); Advantage/XLSoft (~$40–80/producer/mo, annual contracts); State Based Systems (no public pricing) [S]
**Enumerable? YES —** NAIC public License Manager https://external-lookup-web.prod.naic.org/license-manager ; state DOI licensee lookups; Big I / IIABA chapter directories
**Why now:** each state runs its own non-resident regime and complexity is rising — Nebraska adds retaliatory non-resident fees effective **July 2026** [S] agenzee.com. Moderate, not dramatic.
**Moat:** 50-state renewal calendars + NIPR/NAIC data feeds = **per-jurisdiction accumulation (Tier 1)**, plus a buyer segment too small for AgentSync's enterprise motion (Tier 2)
**Hours/wk:** ~8–10 [I]

## 3. Multi-state licence renewal for small debt-collection agencies — US$400–900/mo
**Demand:** operators commonly run in many states, each with its own renewal calendar [S] cornerstonelicensing.com. Adjacent manual service **Integrity Mortgage Licensing charges "as low as $1,150 per state"** [S]. CT Corporation quotes **$89–678+ per state** [S] wolterskluwer.com. A manual cottage industry exists — Orion State Licensing has done this by hand "for over 20 years" [S] collectionlicenses.com
**Competitors:** Harbor Compliance (**software suite $399–799/mo published**, but full-service renewal is quote-only — a reviewer calls it "one of the least transparent parts of their pricing"); Avalara License Management (no public pricing); CT Corporation CLiC [S]
**Enumerable? YES —** NMLS Consumer Access https://nmlsconsumeraccess.org/ ; ACA International directory
**Why now: WEAK — no fresh catalyst found.** Agent flagged this honestly rather than inventing urgency.

## 4. Trust-account three-way reconciliation for small real-estate brokerages — US$300–600/mo
**Demand:** California DRE publishes trust-account handling as its most common audit violation [S] dre.ca.gov PDF. Three-way reconciliation described as the most-cited compliance item in state commission audits and the most common reason brokers are disciplined [S]. Real-estate bookkeeper job ads at **$65k–$100k** requiring IOLTA/trust reconciliation [S] indeed.com
**Competitors:** Loft47 (**$215/mo for 1–10 agents + $900 setup**); SkySlope Books (**custom pricing — quote-only**); Rentvine (~$99/mo, scales per unit) [S]
**Why now: WEAK.** Structural, not news-driven.

## 5. Royalty-compliance audit for SMALL franchisors (5–50 units) — US$400–1,000/mo
**Demand:** the market states its own gap — royalty software is "best for franchisors managing 20–200+ units, **but not ideal for... franchise systems under 5 units**" [S] franfunnel.com. Underreporting reportedly costs franchisors **5–15% of royalty revenue annually**, with some brands recovering **over $1 million** through targeted audits [S] mershimer.com. Manual audit firms (Mershimer, Stonebridge) prove franchisors already pay for this by hand [S]
**Competitors:** FranchiseSoft (**pricing not public, requires consultation**); HelloBooks.ai (no public pricing); ClientTether (enterprise) [S]
**Enumerable? YES —** state FDD registries: California DFPI free FDD search, Wisconsin DFI, Minnesota, Indiana, New York
**Why now: WEAK.**

## 6. IOLTA reconciliation for solo attorneys — **REJECT**
Trust-account allegations were **23% of all California State Bar cases over a decade** [S], and most bars require 30-day reconciliation. But the agent flags this as **the most crowded of its seven**: Clio, CosmoLex, LeanLaw, PCLaw, Soluno. CosmoLex costs a 5-attorney firm **$745/mo**; a LeanLaw+Clio+QBO stack ~**$1,055/mo** [S]. Fails the occupancy test.

## 7. Cyber-insurance readiness documentation for MSPs — **WEAK**
**Over 40% of cyber-insurance claims filed in 2024 were denied**, most often because required controls could not be verified [S] cyberduo.com; insurers moving to third-party verification [S] mind-core.com. **But:** the agent found **NO direct MSP complaint** — demand is inferred from denial statistics. And **vCISO Lite already publishes $299–$1,499/mo** [S] g2.com, so the price band is occupied. MSPs are also a heavily-targeted SaaS channel — the opposite of hard-to-reach.

## Rejected with reasons (the graveyard from this sweep)
- **ISO 17025 lab accreditation / LIMS** — real enterprise pricing (**$50,000+/yr** [S] mastercontrol.com) but **no job ads or forum complaints found**, and it needs deep lab-domain credibility, demos and long sales cycles. Disqualified.
- **DOT/FMCSA driver-qualification files** — already served at **$30–150/mo** (DotFleet, FileFlo, DQM Connect). No pricing gap.
- **Medical provider credentialing** — already served at **$50–200/mo** for 1–5 providers. Gap filled.
- **UK right-to-work checks** — a competitor already at **£149/mo unlimited employees**. Weak gap.
- **Government tender monitoring** — GovWin IQ is quote-only, **but HigherGov ($500–2,500/yr) and GovTribe ($1,350–4,000/yr) already serve the small tier**. Closed.
- **Clinical-trial site eISF** — **Veeva SiteVault has a free tier.** Cannot compete with free from the category leader. Disqualified.
