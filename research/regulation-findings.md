# Regulation-Driven Demand — Findings (2026-09-03)

Evidence tier [S] = web-search summary with URL; [I] = inference. Deadlines marked UNCONFIRMED were not traceable to an official commencement source.

## Strongest: UK Companies House Identity Verification
- Legally live since **18 Nov 2025**; **12-month transition ends 18 Nov 2026** — ~2.5 months away. [S] changestoukcompanylaw.campaign.gov.uk (via 1office.co, Scholes)
- Directors verify by their confirmation-statement date; PSCs submit a code within 14 days. Non-compliance = rejected filings and an **ongoing criminal offence**. [S] figsflow.com
- Competitors already marketing to accountants: ACSPverify, Verify 365, Inform Direct/Bright. ID-check fees £24.99–£74.99 per person. [S] acspverify.com, verify365.app, iconoffices.co.uk
- **Product boundary:** tracking/reminders ONLY. Performing ID verification = passport/selfie = biometric-adjacent regulated data. Avoid.
- Clone resistance **low–moderate** [I]: procedure learnable in days.
- Trust gap: accountants are relationship-driven buyers; a no-name solo NZ vendor faces a credibility problem.
- Steady-state ~2–3 hrs/wk [I]. Low rot (one-off statutory regime).
- **Fatal-ish flaw:** the deadline is a *one-off event*. After 18 Nov 2026 the acute demand collapses into a much smaller steady-state of new incorporations.

## Australia Payday Super
- Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Act 2025, Royal Assent given; **mandatory from 1 July 2026 — already live**. [S] Grant Thornton, ATO, Fair Work Ombudsman
- SG at 12% must land in the fund within **7 business days**; penalty **60% of shortfall** plus daily interest. [S]
- **ATO's Small Business Superannuation Clearing House closed 1 July 2026**, forcing small employers onto third-party clearing houses. [S] superchoiceservices.com
- Competitors: SmallBiz Super Check and FairWork Mate's Super Payslip Audit — **both appear to be solo/small vendors already in this exact niche** [S]. Proof a solo operator can serve it; also proof it is claimed.
- **Biggest threat:** Xero/MYOB/e-PayDay absorbing payday-super compliance as a built-in feature at $10–25/employee/mo [S]. Classic "incumbent ships it free" failure mode.
- Steady-state ~3–4 hrs/wk [I] — SG rates index annually; **at or over the 3 hr ceiling**.
- AU timezone is the operator's best market overlap.

## UK Making Tax Digital for Income Tax — REJECT
- Confirmed law: >£50k from 6 Apr 2026 (live), >£30k Apr 2027, >£20k Apr 2028. HMRC compulsorily enrolling >£50k non-registrants **from Sept 2026**. [S] LITRG, Sage, mtd.digital
- **40+ HMRC-recognised products** already [S] rentalbux.com, including FreeAgent/QuickBooks/Xero/Sage bundling it natively.
- Clone resistance **low**; rots fast (three phase-in dates already); ~4–5 hrs/wk [I] — **breaches the ceiling**. Reject.

## UK Martyn's Law — weak, deadline UNCONFIRMED
- Royal Assent 3 Apr 2025 [S] Walker Morris; Home Office statutory guidance 15 Apr 2026 [S] GOV.UK.
- **DEADLINE UNCONFIRMED** — sources only estimate "spring 2027" / "at least 24 months from Royal Assent". No official commencement order found. Do not build against this date.
- Competitors already live incl. Martyn's Law Software from **£19/mo**, plus a *free* scorecard from Carlisle Support Services [S].
- Life-safety-adjacent liability. Weak.

## Canada CBCA Beneficial Ownership — narrow
- Mandatory since 22 Jan 2024; filings at incorporation, annually, and within 15 days of change. Penalties to **$200,000 and/or 6 months imprisonment**. [S] Doane Grant Thornton
- MinuteKeep already occupies the identical niche [S].
- **Only federally incorporated corporations** — most Canadian businesses incorporate provincially. Market size capped and **NO EVIDENCE FOUND** for the actual count.

## EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency — REJECT
- Transparency obligations live **2 Aug 2026**; penalties to €15M or 3% turnover. [S] European Commission, Cooley
- Compliance cost cited at only **€500–1,500 one-off** [S] — too small and too one-off to sustain a subscription.
- **Legalithm is free through ~April 2028** for EU self-serve AI Act scoping [S] legalithm.com — a free credible incumbent.
- High-risk-tier obligations **delayed to Dec 2027/Aug 2028** [S] DLA Piper — removing the substantial opportunity. Rots fastest of all candidates. Reject.
